# Smashboard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Smashboard is a Chrome extension that draws fun, purely visual effects (cracks, splats, explosions, birds) on top of the web page you are viewing.

**We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.**

- Smashboard has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
- It makes no network requests and sends nothing to any server.
- It does not read, copy or change the content of the pages you visit. Effects are drawn on a separate transparent layer and disappear when you reload the page or press Repair.
- The Screenshot button captures the visible tab only when you click it. The image stays on your device and is offered to you as a download. It is never uploaded anywhere.
- All sound effects are generated locally in your browser.

**Permissions:** `activeTab` and `scripting` are used only to run the effects on the page where you click the extension icon, and to take a screenshot when you ask for one.
